It does the work that comes
after the edit.

Made for long-form talk: interviews, lectures, sermons, podcasts. The hours spent trimming pauses one by one and typing subtitles by hand go away.

It removes only the silence and the filler

It finds the gaps between sentences, the “um” and “uh”, and the false starts you re-took. You choose how tight the cut is, and you see every cut in the transcript before it is applied.

  • Preview silence and filler cuts before applying
  • Clear out re-taken false starts
  • Give it a target length and it picks sentences to fit. The spoken words are never rewritten

Subtitles break where the meaning breaks

Not at a fixed character count. It reads the whole sentence, picks the most readable break points, and times each subtitle to the word. Subtitles are generated as native Final Cut Pro titles, so they drop straight onto the timeline.

  • Generated with your own Final Cut Pro title template
  • Adjust size, length, two-line layout and vertical position
  • Also exports SRT, TXT and Markdown

Edit video like a document.

No export step

No rendering, no separate audio export. Drag the project you are editing onto the window and it reads only the audio your timeline uses. Multicam, compound and synchronized clips work as they are.

Your original stays untouched

The result is always a new project. The source XML and media are never modified, and every edit can be undone.

It asks before it corrects

Add names and terms to the dictionary and they stay consistent across every subtitle. Corrections found from context are shown as a list, and only the ones you approve are applied.

Your footage never
leaves this Mac.

Speech recognition, correction, subtitle splitting and length fitting all run on Apple Silicon. There is no server, so there is no upload to wait for and no footage to hand over. The speech model downloads once.

Drop it in, edit, send it back.

  1. Import

    Drag a Final Cut Pro project or .fcpxmld anywhere onto the window.

  2. Analyze

    Pick options on the left and start. You see each stage and the time remaining.

  3. Edit script and subtitles

    Delete words to cut the video, split subtitles, and set the style.

  4. Back to Final Cut Pro

    Send the result as a new project. Re-exporting takes seconds, with no re-analysis.
